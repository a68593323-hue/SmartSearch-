Questions, answered

Quick answers before you start.

Which syllabus does this cover?

AKTU B.Tech CSE subjects across all semesters. Codes and topics can vary by batch, so always check the official AKTU syllabus.

How is difficulty calculated?

It is computed from syllabus topic tags and shown as a theory, coding, numerical and practical split for each subject.

Where do the notes and videos come from?

They link out to public sources such as GeeksforGeeks and YouTube channels like Gate Smashers and KnowledgeGATE. Some cards are search links rather than a single exact video.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Google or email is optional and only keeps your name and study streak on this device.
